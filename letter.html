CHAPTER FOUR

A message, folded with love

A letter for my baby.

A letter for the birthday of the woman who holds my heart. 💙🌻

My dearest Maria,

If I were given an eternity to write about you, I fear even that would not be enough. For how could a mere collection of words ever capture someone as wonderful as you? How could ink possibly describe the warmth you bring into my life, the happiness you give me, or the countless reasons I find myself falling in love with you over and over again?

Today, the world celebrates the day you were born. But if I may be selfish for just a moment, I would like to celebrate something else: the extraordinary privilege of being alive in the same lifetime as you.

Happy birthday, my love.

Sometimes, I find myself thinking about how our story began. Two strangers, living their own separate lives, having absolutely no idea that somewhere along the way, they would become such an important part of each other's worlds.

Who would have thought that a simple dating app called Litmatch would lead me to someone I would eventually hold so close to my heart? Of all the conversations I could have started, all the people I could have met, and all the paths I could have taken, somehow, I found you.

And I wouldn't trade that moment for anything.

I still think about that late night when we first exchanged those three little words. When I told you I loved you, and you said it back. It might have seemed like a simple exchange of messages on a screen, but to me, it became something I would carry in my heart for a very long time.

I wish I could go back to that very moment, just to tell the younger version of myself that the girl on the other side of that screen would one day become someone so precious to him.

That he would miss her, worry about her, laugh with her, dream about a future with her, and find himself writing an entire letter just to remind her how much she means to him.

And if I could relive that night a thousand times, I would still choose to say those words.

I love you, Maria.

And I hope you never grow tired of hearing me say that.

I love you not only for the beautiful person you are, but for the little things that make you you. Your laughter, your smile, your personality, your imperfections, your dreams, and even the things you might sometimes struggle to love about yourself.

I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even for just a moment. Perhaps then you would understand why I consider myself so fortunate. Perhaps you would finally understand why, even on an ordinary day, the thought of you is enough to make my heart feel a little fuller.

You do not have to be perfect to be loved by me. You do not have to have everything figured out. You do not have to pretend to be strong when you're tired, nor hide your sadness when life becomes difficult.

You are allowed to have bad days. You are allowed to make mistakes. You are allowed to rest.

And through all of it, I hope you remember that you deserve kindness, patience, understanding, and a love that never makes you question your worth.

I know life isn't always easy. I know there are days when school becomes exhausting, responsibilities become overwhelming, and the world seems to demand more from you than you can give. But Maria, please don't ever think that you are falling behind simply because you need time to breathe.

You are growing, learning, and becoming the person you are meant to be. And I am so incredibly proud of you.

I want you to know that you never have to earn my admiration by accomplishing something extraordinary. I am already proud of you simply for continuing, even on the days when continuing is difficult.

I want to be there for your victories, no matter how small they may seem. I want to celebrate every achievement, listen to every story, and comfort you when things don't go as planned.

I want to be someone you can come home to emotionally, someone you can speak to without fear of being judged, someone who listens not just to respond, but because what you have to say genuinely matters to me.

And I want to keep learning how to love you properly.

I know that loving someone is not simply about saying beautiful words. It is about the little things we do when nobody is watching. It is about patience when misunderstandings happen, listening when the other person needs to be heard, and understanding that love requires effort even on the days when things aren't easy.

I know I am not perfect. There will be times when I make mistakes, when I fail to understand something, or when I don't express myself as well as I should. But I don't want to use my imperfections as an excuse to stop growing.

I want to become a better man, not because I believe I must be perfect to deserve you, but because I want to give you the kind of love that makes you feel safe, appreciated, respected, and cherished.

I want my presence to bring you peace, not pressure. I want you to feel that you can be yourself around me, without having to pretend to be someone else.

And now, my love, there is something I want to tell you about the future.

I dream of marrying you someday.

I dream of a day when I won't have to say goodnight through a screen, when I can look into your eyes and tell you how much I love you without a distance separating us. I dream of the day I can hold your hand, stand beside you, and promise you my love in front of the people who matter to us.

I dream of calling you my wife.

Perhaps we'll look back at this letter years from now. Perhaps we'll laugh at how young we were, how nervous we might have been, and how everything began with a simple conversation on Litmatch.

Maybe we'll be sitting beside each other, reading these words again, and I'll turn to you with the biggest smile because the future I once wrote about has finally become our present.

I want to experience the ordinary things with you, too. Waking up knowing you're beside me, sharing meals, making silly jokes, going on little adventures, and finding happiness in the simplest moments.

I don't need a perfect life. I don't need a life without difficulties. I simply hope that whatever life brings us, we continue to face it with love, understanding, and the willingness to choose each other.

I cannot promise that every day will be beautiful. But I can promise that I will keep learning, keep growing, and keep doing my best to love you in ways that you can actually feel.

I want to grow old with you, Maria. I want to see all the versions of you that time will bring. The dreams you'll accomplish, the person you'll become, the places you'll visit, and the happiness you'll discover along the way.

And if life allows me that privilege, I want to be there through every chapter.

If I could give you one gift that would last beyond today, it would be the ability to see just how much your existence matters.

You are more than your achievements, more than your responsibilities, and more than the expectations placed upon you.

You are Maria.

And that alone is something worth celebrating.

So, on your birthday, my love, I wish you happiness that doesn't disappear when the candles are blown out. I wish you courage when life becomes difficult, opportunities that bring you closer to your dreams, and countless reasons to smile even on the most ordinary days.

I wish you a lifetime of discovering how wonderful you truly are.

Thank you for being born.

Thank you for finding your way into my life.

Thank you for that first conversation, that first confession of love, and every little memory that has brought us to this moment.

And most importantly, thank you for being you.

Out of all the people I could have met, all the stories I could have lived, and all the paths my life could have taken, I am endlessly grateful that one of them led me to you.

If I could ask the universe for one more beautiful thing, it would be that I get to witness many more of your birthdays. Not just as someone who sends you a letter, but as someone who gets to stand beside you, celebrate you, and remind you in person how extraordinary you are.

Happy birthday, my beautiful Maria.

May the world be as gentle with you as I wish I could be every single day. May your heart never forget how deserving you are of happiness. And may every year ahead remind you that your story is still being written, with so many beautiful chapters yet to come.

And if someday, many years from now, you ever wonder whether the boy who wrote this letter truly meant every word, I hope you will look at the life we've built and find your answer there.

Because I don't just want to write about loving you.

I want to spend my life showing you.

I loved you when you were a stranger on the other side of a screen. I love you now, as the woman who holds my heart. And if I am fortunate enough to have all the tomorrows I dream of, I will love you in every single one of them.

Forever your admirer, your biggest supporter, your future husband, and the boy who will always be grateful that he met you.

With all the love my heart can hold,

Your Andrei. 💙🌻
